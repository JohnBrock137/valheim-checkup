How progress is read, catalog notes, and save locations

Achievement credit uses Valheim’s credited action records. Lifetime actions includes actions that may not have earned achievement credit. Neither view changes your game or unlocks Steam achievements. The Master Hunter uses the Hard record; the same creature list is also available for The Hunter at any difficulty.

Undiscovered means the mapped item is absent from your character’s known recipes or materials and has no matching action in the selected record. It does not mean the achievement is hidden. The game may have recipe prerequisites this tool does not evaluate.

Unverified means the workbook name cannot be reliably matched to a save identifier, or multiple variants share its name. It is separate from Missing. Open Details, double-click, or right-click a row to compare records, add a private note, or apply a manual correction. The explicit Details button also works on touchscreens. Corrections, notes, and stars stay with that character in this browser and can be exported.

The catalog comes from the community Valheim: Achievements Tracker workbook: 108 creatures, 68 trophies, 82 cooked dishes, 415 crafting entries, 400 building entries, and 97 edible items, plus 12 fish. Counts follow this workbook and may differ after game updates. Duplicate workbook entries are retained. The Bronze duplicate shares one record; ambiguous roof variants remain unverified.

English names are matched using Jötunn’s localization reference. The importer’s field order and difficulty records were checked against Valheim’s game files. It supports profiles 46–47, player data 33–34, and inventories 109–110. Damaged or unsupported saves produce an error without replacing your current progress.

Steam Cloud saves can be downloaded from Steam’s Valheim cloud files. Local characters are usually under %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\IronGate\Valheim\characters_local. Use a recent .fch, not a world .db or a backup you don’t intend to inspect.

Only extracted checklist records, discovered-item lists, pins, and corrections are remembered locally; the original file is never uploaded.
